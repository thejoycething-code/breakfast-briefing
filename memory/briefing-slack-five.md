Step 10 of [[breakfast-briefing]] posts the edition to #campaigns-en-gb (`C9RH217PZ`) with the
top five stories. Chris replaced three of five on the first run (25.08.2026): he cut France
enacting its assisted dying law (non-UK), swapped Right To Life UK's "2 weeks left" campaign
update for Politico Europe's analysis of the same bill, and added two immigration/Islam stories
from the Telegraph and GB News.

**Why:** the Doc and the Slack five answer different questions. The Doc asks *who published the
document* — which is why [[briefing-cluster-provenance]] makes an advocacy body's own release
beat a newsroom's write-up. The Slack five asks *what will a GB campaigner actually read*, and
there a ★ primary source has no automatic claim on a slot.

**How to apply:** UK stories only. Prefer national-press reporting with campaign bite over an
advocacy org's own campaign update. Immigration and Islam in Britain are core beats for this
channel, not edge cases. Open on the live legislative fight, close on the sharpest single story
— the list is not ranked by scale or corroboration count.
