**Decision (Christopher, 6 Sept 2026):** the devolved chambers remain a section
of the Monday Westminster edition, headed **"Scotland, Wales and Northern
Ireland"** (was "Devolved" — said where, not what). No standalone devolved
editions.

**Why:** measured, a standalone edition the week of 31 Aug would have carried
**two items** (1 Senedd, 1 NI, 0 Holyrood scored 2+); the store holds ~103
digest-worthy devolved items ever (51/34/18). The EU earns its own 3,287-word
edition because ten sections have content; the devolved three would be
headings. Also structural: the devolved weeklies are watching briefs with no
secrets and no publish step — an "edition" implies a reader and changes that.

**Revisit:** mid-October 2026, after four or five sitting weeks with the
devolved judge running (wired into the Monday publish 6 Sept). Test: if the
section carries 10+ items a week consistently, split; if ~3, leave it.
