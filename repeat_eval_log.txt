--- correction to the entry above -------------------------------------------------------
The "VETO: 2 FAILING" line above was a fault in this harness, not in the fix it was scoring.
Both failures were artefacts, found and fixed the same hour:
  1. gold_pairs() left run_tests' "::entity::" marker inside the past headline, so the word
     "entity" entered the comparison. One phantom failure.
  2. The remaining case (Illinois assisted suicide) asserts the ENTITY ARM fires, which
     depends on a token's document frequency in whatever corpus is loaded - "illinoi" clears
     the gate in production's corpus and not in this eval's. A veto has to be
     corpus-independent, so ::entity:: cases are now excluded from GOLD and left to
     run_tests, which checks them at pair level.
Re-run after both fixes, same code under test:
